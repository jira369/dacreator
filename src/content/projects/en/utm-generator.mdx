## Purpose

The generator builds UTM links according to the rightmart briefing for ten channels. It replaces the generator overview in the marketing data atlas and the Google Sheet.

## Channels

Paid: general, Meta, TikTok, SEA. Owned and partner: CRM, affiliate, cooperation, influencer. Organic and offline: SEO, OOH with QR codes.

## Rules

- The full rule set lives in one configuration, each line maps to one field.
- Free fields are lowercased, spaces become hyphens, only ASCII characters are allowed.
- Platform macros such as click IDs are only set where they belong.
- A missing required field triggers a notice.

The repository is internal, so there is no link.
