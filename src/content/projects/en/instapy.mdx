## Overview

**InstaPy** is an open-source Instagram automation tool that I contributed to and utilized for growth hacking experiments. Built with Python, it enables automated Instagram interactions with intelligent targeting capabilities.

## Features

- **Smart Targeting** — Define audience parameters based on hashtags, locations, and user profiles
- **Automated Interactions** — Likes, follows, unfollows, and comments with natural timing
- **Safety Limits** — Built-in rate limiting and randomization to mimic human behavior
- **Analytics** — Track growth metrics and engagement rates over time
- **Proxy Support** — Rotate proxies for distributed automation

## What I Learned

This project was an early exploration into growth hacking and automation. It taught me valuable lessons about:

- Platform APIs and their limitations
- The balance between automation and authenticity
- Building sustainable growth strategies vs. short-term hacks
- Open-source community collaboration

These insights directly influenced my approach to ethical, sustainable digital marketing strategies.
