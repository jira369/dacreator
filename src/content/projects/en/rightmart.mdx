## Overview

**rightmart** is one of Germany's leading legal tech companies, providing accessible legal services through digital channels. I joined in March 2023 after 2 years of consulting rightmart from the agency side at 42DIGITAL — and haven't looked back.

## My Journey at rightmart

### Team Lead Social Media (2024 — Present)
Leading a team across paid & organic social media, responsible for the full social media strategy:

- **Team Leadership** — Managing a team of 7 across paid social, organic social, and creative
- **Cross-functional Project Management** — Leading interdisciplinary projects using Scrum & Kanban
- **Landing Page Optimization** — Deep involvement in CVR optimization, web design and development
- **Influencer Marketing** — Building and managing influencer partnerships
- **External Agency Management** — Steering relationships with external service providers
- **Creative Strategy** — Developing creatives and strategic direction for social media campaigns

### Performance Marketing Manager (2023 — 2024)
Drove growth across all paid channels:

- **Paid Social** — Meta Ads, TikTok Ads campaign management and optimization
- **Paid Search** — Google Ads, Microsoft Ads
- **Native Ads** — Taboola, Criteo, rtb house, tisoomi
- **Tracking** — GTM, server-side tracking implementation
- **CRO** — Landing page creation, A/B testing funnels
- **New Brand Building** — Built and launched new satellite brands
- **Organic Social** — Professionalized the organic social media presence
