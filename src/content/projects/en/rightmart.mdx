## Overview

**rightmart** is one of the leading legal tech companies in Germany, offering accessible legal services through digital channels. I joined in March 2023 after advising rightmart for two years on the agency side at 42DIGITAL.

## Result

From low seven-figure revenue to 60 million a year through performance marketing. Plus the launch of sportwetten-kanzlei.de including influencer marketing in four months: about 3 million euros of spend, over 40 million euros in dispute value.

## My path at rightmart

- **Full ownership:** leads and cases across all paid channels and legal areas, with targets for CPL, CPS and CAC
- **Channel mix:** Google, Bing, Meta, TikTok, plus piloting and scaling new channels
- **Tracking and data:** server-side tracking, health checks, GA4 for every group domain, marketing data atlas
- **AI-first:** apps built with Claude for CRO, SEO, creative strategy, link building and brand
- **Leadership:** 2 direct and 5 indirect reports, agreeing goals instead of handing out tasks
