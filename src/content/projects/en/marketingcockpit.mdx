## Purpose

The cockpit answers every day: are we on target this month for spend, paid leads and CPL, and where does someone need to step in? It joins the cleaned marketing export with the target sheet of the channel budget plan and the changelog.

## Principles

- **Target vs. actual first:** every metric sits next to its target and pace.
- **Deviation before completeness:** what needs action is on top and carries color.
- **Traceable numbers:** corrections, maturing and data cut-off are visible, nothing is silently replaced.
- **No invented values:** when something is missing, a placeholder and the reason are shown.

## Pages

Month status, pace matrix by legal area and channel, trends, budget split, platform pages for Google, Meta and TikTok, CRM vs. interface leads, attribution model, data quality, pins and changelog.
